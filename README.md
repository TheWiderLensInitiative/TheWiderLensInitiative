### 🟣 Clara: your private AI assistant
A free AI assistant that runs on **your own PC**, with an Android app to talk to her. She researches, browses, sets
reminders, talks hands-free, asks for your help at CAPTCHAs and waits for your OK before anything risky. Your chats,
files and memories never leave your computer.
